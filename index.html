The little jobs that keep a home looked after, remembered for you.

Filters, smoke alarms, the boiler service, descaling the kettle. HomePing keeps track of what's due, reminds you gently, and records what you've done.

Repeating jobs, daily to yearly, with a reminder on the day. Postpone one occurrence without moving the whole schedule.

A clear Today view

Rooms and appliances

Brands, models, purchase dates and warranty countdowns, with manuals and receipts kept alongside.

A history you can trust

Every completed job with notes and what it cost, so you know when it was last done.

Ready-made suggestions

A library of common maintenance tasks, with seasonal ideas, to get started in a couple of minutes.

On all your iPhones

Syncs through your own private iCloud, and works fully offline.

Private by design

No account, no HomePing server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your home's details stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud, which the developer can't access. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with an optional Pro

HomePing is free to use, with up to 10 appliances and 3 photos or documents per item. HomePing Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, that adds unlimited appliances and attachments, cost reports and appliance-specific suggestions. It can be shared with your Family Sharing group.

Suggested intervals are a starting point. Always follow your manufacturer's guidance and local requirements.
